About OptionsWizard

A free education site for options traders — from your first contract to systematic premium selling.

What this site is

OptionsWizard is a free, independent educational resource about options trading — with an emphasis on mechanical, probability-based premium selling: the approach of systematically selling options when implied volatility is rich, sizing every position small, and managing each trade by fixed rules instead of gut feel.

There is nothing for sale here: no courses, no trade signals, no broker pitches, no hype. The site is funded by advertising, which is why you'll see ad slots on the pages — the content itself will always stay free.

Who it's for

📘 Complete beginners

Options Basics starts from zero — what a contract is, calls vs puts, strike, expiration, and premium — then The Greeks teaches the five numbers that drive every option's price.

♟ Active premium sellers

The Playbook lays out nine premium-selling structures with exact setups and management rules, The Rules gives the sixteen mechanical rules, and Metrics covers the dashboard behind every entry decision.

Our approach

Numbers over narratives. Every strategy on this site is presented the same way — setup, when to use it, and exactly how it's managed — because in premium selling, the management is the edge: take winners at 50% of max profit, exit at 21 days to expiration, size small enough to survive the losing streaks.

Not financial advice: everything here is educational content only — not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Options trading involves substantial risk of loss and isn't suitable for everyone. Talk to a qualified financial professional before trading real money.

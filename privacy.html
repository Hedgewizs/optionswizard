Privacy Policy

What we collect (almost nothing), and how advertising cookies work on this site.

Overview

OptionsWizard is a static educational website. We don't run user accounts, logins, newsletters, comment sections, or contact forms — so there's very little here that could identify you.

Information we collect

Directly: none. We don't ask for or store your name, email address, or any other personal data. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may record standard server logs — such as IP addresses and browser types — that are technically necessary to serve the pages. That's their infrastructure logging, not our database; we have no access to it and build no profiles from it.

Cookies and advertising

To keep this site free, we display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners (including DoubleClick) may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other websites — this is known as interest-based or personalized advertising.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites like this one at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • You can also opt out of many third-party advertising cookies at optout.aboutads.info.

Analytics

We don't currently use web analytics tools. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

External links

Links to third-party websites (for example, Google's policy pages) are governed by those sites' own privacy policies, not this one.

Children

This is a general-audience educational resource about financial markets. It is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves — for example, when advertising is activated. The current version is always the one on this page.

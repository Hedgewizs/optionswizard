The Core Philosophy

Four ideas do almost all the work. Everything on this site is a consequence of them.

1. Sell premium

Options are systematically priced for larger moves than actually happen — implied volatility runs richer than realized volatility over time. A consistent seller of options collects that difference. Volatility isn't the enemy; it's the product.

2. Probability over opinion

Pick trades by their probability of profit, not by a forecast about direction. Buying stock is roughly a coin flip. Selling premium when implied volatility is elevated can start at 60–90% probability of profit — before any view on the market.

3. Trade small, trade often

The edge is small per trade, so occurrences are the engine. A high number of small, consistent trades lets the law of large numbers do the compounding. One hero trade is gambling; a hundred disciplined ones is a business.

4. Manage winners early

Every trade gets its management plan before entry — where you'll take profit, when you'll exit, how you'll defend. No discretion, no hope, no "let me watch it for one more day."

The Flagship Formula

The standard mechanical setup for short-premium trades. Five numbers, memorized once, applied forever:

1
16Δ
Sell the 16-delta strikes — about one standard deviation out
2
45
Enter with ~45 days to expiration
3
50+
Only when IV Rank is above 50
4
50%
Take profit at 50% of max profit
5
21
Close or roll at 21 days to expiration — always
Why it works: large-sample backtests of 16-delta, 45-DTE short strangles on SPY show that managing winners at 50% of max profit produces roughly a 90% win rate with an average hold around three weeks — while exiting at 21 DTE cuts the largest losses roughly in half versus holding to expiration. The management is the edge.
